# REBUILT HUB side vinyl

Artwork: FIRST Robotics Competition, REBUILT presented by Haas, drawing **GE-26327 — Hub Side Panel Plastic and Vinyl**.

Source: https://firstfrc.blob.core.windows.net/frc2026/FieldAssets/2026-field-dwg-game-specific.pdf (page 131).

The PNG is a direct rendering of the artwork in the drawing's orthographic front view, including the original logo, sponsor credit, teal field, and dashed border. It is not a generated or redrawn logo. The original print file is identified in the drawing as `GE-26327-Deco.eps`.

Extraction: `pdftoppm -f 131 -singlefile -scale-to 4096 -x 658 -y 1077 -W 1482 -H 960 -png 2026-field-dwg-game-specific.pdf rebuilt-hub-banner`.

The decal geometry clips the lower V to the printed outline, excluding the mounting holes visible below the artwork in the drawing. Width 39.64 in; height 25.61 in; top offset 14.53 in below the 1.273175 m CAD panel top. Artwork belongs to FIRST.
