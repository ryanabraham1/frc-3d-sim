# REBUILT reconstruction references

4414: [team technical binder](https://2026.team4414.com/), including the Robot, Shooter, Turret, Intake, and Dye Rotor CAD renders. Ryan also supplied `f1a062d4-8f3d-4c91-9301-538bd60a322a.glb`, an image-generated mesh. The supplied mesh is preserved as `public/robots/2026/hightide-generated-reference.glb` and can be inspected in `/tools/generated-model-review.html`.

The supplied mesh reinforces the clipped fixed-end corners, broad horizontal hopper shoulder, and wide pancake turret deck. Those silhouettes guide the independent animated model. The generated file is one fused mesh without mechanism groups or materials; the game uses separate pocketed rails, turret deck, hood, intake carriage, and rotor so each mechanism can move and FUEL can occupy the open hopper. Dimensions and mechanism behavior are fitted against the team's binder rather than inferred from generated surface wrinkles.

1690: [team CAD release](https://www.chiefdelphi.com/t/frc-orbit-1690-2026-robot-cad-release/520673), particularly the side-by-side robot photograph and CAD render. Kepler's model follows the large trapezoidal bridge windows and curved turret clearance, hollow side-guided hood, three centered compliant wheels, small black pocketed gears, carbon side walls, large intake-face X cutouts, exposed leading shaft, and separate friction kicker.

These are visual reconstructions, not dimensionally exact exports of either team's complete CAD. Gameplay capacities, rates, frame dimensions, and shot tuning remain in their existing configs.
